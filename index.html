AC care through the year in Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar

Summers here are long and hot, and the monsoon brings damp air. A little care at the right time keeps your AC reliable.

Before the hot months

Book an AC service, wash the filters and make sure the outdoor unit has clear space around it. This is also the time to book installation, before everyone else does.

Peak summer

Wash the filters every few weeks. Do not ignore weak cooling, ice or dripping water: small faults grow fast when the AC runs all day.

Monsoon

Damp air makes drain blockages and musty smells more common. Running the AC in fan mode for a while can help dry the coil. If a smell stays, book a deep clean.

Winter, when the AC rests

Switch the AC off at the wall. Before the next season, get it serviced so you start the summer with a clean, tested AC.

Repair or replace? Our honest view

Usually worth repairing

  • One failed part: capacitor, fan motor, sensor, relay, belt, door seal
  • A leak at a joint
  • An appliance that was working well before this fault

Think before repairing

  • A new compressor on a very old AC or fridge
  • Worn drum bearings plus a failed board on an old washing machine
  • The same appliance failing again and again in different places

How much does AC repair cost in Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar?

Why is my AC running but not cooling?

Common causes are dirty filters or coils, low gas from a leak, a weak capacitor, a failing compressor, or a faulty sensor or board. A technician needs to inspect it.

How often should I service my AC?

At least once a year, ideally before summer. Every six months if the AC runs most of the day or the area is dusty.

What is AC deep cleaning?

A jet pump wash of the indoor and outdoor coils and the blower. It suits an AC that smells, has weak airflow after a service, or has not been cleaned for years.

Do you repair inverter ACs?
