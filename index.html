AC repair, service and installation in Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar

Summers in Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar (Aurangabad) are hot and dusty, and an AC that struggles in April or May makes life miserable. Most AC problems start small: a clogged filter, a blocked drain, a weak capacitor. Catching them early costs less than waiting for a breakdown.

AC repair: when your AC is already giving trouble

If your AC is not cooling, leaking water, making noise or refusing to start, you need a repair visit, not a routine service. Our technician checks the unit, finds the cause and tells you the cost before starting. See our AC repair page for the faults we handle.

AC service: before trouble starts

A yearly AC service cleans the filters and coils, checks the drain, electrical parts and gas pressure, and tests cooling. Book it before summer. If your AC runs most of the day, every six months is sensible.

AC deep cleaning: when a normal service is not enough

If the AC smells musty or airflow stays weak after a service, jet pump deep cleaning washes the coils and blower properly. Not sure which you need? Read deep cleaning vs normal service.

AC installation: get it right the first time

Poor installation causes weak cooling, noise and leaks. We fit split and window units and shift existing ACs, with proper mounting, piping, drain and a test run. Details on AC installation.

Frequently asked questions

What causes an AC to stop cooling?

Common causes are dirty filters or coils, low refrigerant from a leak, a failing capacitor or compressor, and a faulty sensor or PCB. A technician needs to inspect the unit to confirm which.

How often should an AC be serviced?

Once a year at minimum, ideally before summer. Heavily used ACs, or ACs in dusty areas, benefit from servicing every six months.

How much does AC repair cost in Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar?

It depends on the fault and the parts. A clean or capacitor change costs far less than a compressor or PCB replacement. We inspect first and tell you the cost before any repair starts.
