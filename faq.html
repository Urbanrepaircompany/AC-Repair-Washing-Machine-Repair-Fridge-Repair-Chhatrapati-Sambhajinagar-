A new AC usually needs only filter washing in the first year. A full service helps after a season of heavy use or if the AC was installed in a dusty place.

Yes. Switch the AC off, wash the filter, dry it fully and refit it. Leave the coil and outdoor unit to a technician.

AC gas refilling

How much does AC gas refilling cost in Aurangabad?

It depends on the AC type, the refrigerant and whether a leak must be repaired first. We inspect, find the cause and quote before any work.

Does AC gas run out on its own?

No. AC gas is sealed in the system and is not used up. If it is low, there is a leak.

How often does an AC need gas?

A healthy AC should not need gas at all. If you need to refill it every season, there is a leak that should be repaired.

What are the signs of low AC gas?

Weak cooling, ice on the pipe or outdoor valve, a hissing sound, an oily pipe joint, or an AC that runs non-stop without cooling.

Can I run my AC with low gas?

It is better not to. Low gas strains the compressor and can lead to a much bigger repair.

How long does AC gas refilling take?

Checking and refilling usually takes a short visit. If a leak must be repaired, it takes longer. The technician tells you after inspection.

Is gas refilling part of an AC service?

No. A service checks the gas pressure. If gas is low, we repair the leak and refill as a separate job, with a quote first.

Which gas does my AC need: R22, R32 or R410A?

The refrigerant type is on the outdoor unit's label. Each AC must be refilled with its own type. Tell us the model and we will confirm.

Do you repair gas leaks?

Yes. We find the leak, repair it, vacuum the system and then refill.

Why is my washing machine not draining?

Common causes are a blocked drain hose or filter, a failed drain pump or a control fault. Unplug it and check the hose and the drain filter first.

Why is my washing machine not spinning?

A worn belt or motor, a faulty door or lid switch, or an unbalanced load can stop the spin. Rebalance the load first. If it still fails, it needs a technician.

Why does my washing machine shake or move?

The machine may be unlevel, overloaded or unbalanced, or its suspension or shock absorbers may be worn. Level it and redistribute the load. If it continues, call us.

Why is water leaking from my washing machine?

Common causes are a worn door seal, a loose or cracked hose, or a pump seal. We find the source and replace the part.

Why does my washing machine smell?

Damp detergent residue builds up in the drum and seal. Leave the door open after washes and run a drum clean. If the smell stays, a drain blockage may be the cause.

What does an error code on my washing machine mean?

Codes differ by brand, but most point to drain, door lock, water inlet or load balance problems. Check your manual, and call us with the code and model if it does not clear.

Does a refrigerator need gas refilling?

A refrigerator uses sealed gas and does not use it up. If it is low there is a leak, which has to be repaired before refilling.

Why is my fridge warm but my freezer cold?

This often points to an evaporator fan or defrost problem that stops cold air reaching the fridge section. It needs a technician.

Why is my refrigerator making a loud noise?
