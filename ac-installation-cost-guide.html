AC Installation Cost: What Affects the Price?

By Urban Repair Company · 18 October 2026

No honest installer can give an exact price without knowing your setup. These factors decide the cost:

  • Split or window AC
  • Distance between indoor and outdoor units (pipe length)
  • Wall type and mounting stand needs
  • Extra wiring, a new power point or stabiliser
  • Drain routing
  • New installation or shifting an old AC

Send us your AC type, room position and a photo of the outdoor wall, and we will quote.
