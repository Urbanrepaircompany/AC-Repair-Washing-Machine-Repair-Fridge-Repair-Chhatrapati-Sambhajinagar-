Common questions

What causes an AC to stop cooling?

Common causes are dirty filters or coils, low refrigerant from a leak, a failing capacitor or compressor, and a faulty sensor or PCB. A technician must inspect the unit to confirm which.

How much does AC repair cost?

It depends on the fault and parts. Cleaning or a capacitor change costs far less than a compressor or PCB replacement. We give an estimate after inspection, before any work.
